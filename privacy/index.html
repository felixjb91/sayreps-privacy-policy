Privacy Policy – SayReps

Effective date: 2026-04-18  ·  Last updated: 2026-05-02

This Privacy Policy applies to the SayReps mobile application (hereinafter referred to as "Application"), developed by Jean-Baptiste Félix (hereinafter referred to as "Service Provider"). This service is provided as a freemium product and is offered "AS IS".

1. Information You Provide

When you create an account, the Application collects your email address and a hashed password. Registration is required to access certain features of the Application. The Service Provider may use your contact information to send you important service-related notices. Marketing communications will only be sent with your explicit consent, and you may opt out at any time.

2. Information Collected Automatically

The Application may automatically collect certain technical information, including the type of mobile device you use, your device's unique identifier, IP address, mobile operating system, browser type, and usage patterns within the Application. This data is collected to help us maintain, improve, and secure the Application.

3. Microphone and Voice Data

The Application requests access to your device's microphone to enable voice-driven workout logging. When you use the voice input feature, your speech is captured and securely transmitted over HTTPS to our servers for transcription.

  • Raw audio is not stored — only the resulting transcribed text is saved to your account.
  • Audio data is never shared with third parties for advertising purposes.
  • You can revoke microphone access at any time via your device's system settings without affecting other features of the Application.

4. Location Data

The Application does not collect precise real-time location information from your device.

5. Third-Party Services

The Application relies on the following third-party services to operate. Each has its own privacy policy governing how it handles data:

  • Supabase — database and authentication, hosted in the European Union
  • OpenAI — voice transcription (Whisper) and natural language processing (GPT). Your voice data is transmitted to OpenAI solely to provide the transcription feature and is not used to train OpenAI models.
  • PostHog — product analytics and usage tracking, used to understand how users interact with the Application and to improve the product experience
  • Expo — mobile application infrastructure
  • Google Play Services

The Service Provider may also disclose your information when required by law, when necessary to protect the rights or safety of users, or in response to a valid government request.

6. Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored securely on Supabase infrastructure located in the European Union, in compliance with the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR). The Service Provider implements physical, electronic, and procedural safeguards to protect your information. Access is limited to authorized personnel who need it to operate or improve the Application. No security system can guarantee absolute protection against all potential breaches.

7. Data Retention

User-provided data is retained for as long as your account is active and for a reasonable period thereafter. Automatically collected analytics data may be retained for up to 24 months, after which it may be aggregated and anonymized.

8. Your Rights and Account Deletion

You have the right to access, correct, or delete your personal data at any time. To request deletion of your account and all associated data, please contact us at support@sayreps.com. We will process your request within a reasonable timeframe. Please note that some data may be required for the Application to function properly and will be deleted upon account termination.

Note: In-app account deletion is planned for a future update of the Application.

9. Children's Privacy

The Application is not directed at children under the age of 13. The Service Provider does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If we become aware that a child under 13 has provided personal information, we will delete it immediately. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has submitted information to us, please contact us at support@sayreps.com.

10. Opt-Out

You may stop all data collection by uninstalling the Application using the standard uninstall process available on your device or through the app marketplace.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes by updating the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. Continued use of the Application after changes have been posted constitutes your acceptance of the updated policy.

12. Contact

If you have any questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at:
support@sayreps.com
